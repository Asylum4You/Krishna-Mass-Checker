Classification describes provider-reported metadata quality only. It does not predict card validity, funds, 3-D Secure enrollment, authentication, or payment success. Results may be incomplete or outdated.

Important: BIN/IIN data cannot establish whether an individual card is active, funded, enrolled in 3-D Secure, exempt from a challenge, or capable of completing a transaction. No payment, authorization, or authentication request is made.

Privacy by design: input is limited in the browser to 6–8 digits and is not saved by this page. The 6–8 digit prefix is sent to the public metadata provider. When hosted with the included Python server, the server proxies and caches the metadata request.
